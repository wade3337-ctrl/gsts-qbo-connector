Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Great Scott Tree Service internal QuickBooks integration ("the Integration") handles data. The Integration is a private tool operated by Great Scott Tree Service, Inc. ("we", "us") solely for our own internal use.

Who this applies to

The Integration is used only by authorized Great Scott Tree Service personnel against our own Intuit/QuickBooks company. It is not distributed to, or used by, any third party or member of the public.

What data we access

Through Intuit's official API, the Integration reads accounting data from our own QuickBooks Online / Intuit Enterprise Suite company — including customers, vendors, invoices, bills, accounts receivable/payable, and financial reports. Access is read-only.

How data is used

Data is used exclusively for internal financial reporting, reconciliation, and analysis within Great Scott Tree Service. We do not sell, rent, or share this data with any third party.

How data is stored and secured

API credentials (OAuth client secret and refresh token) are stored on a private, access-controlled company server with file permissions restricted to the operating account. Network access to internal services is restricted to our private network. We retain only what is needed for the reporting functions above.

Data deletion & disconnection

Access can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the app from within Intuit, or by contacting us. Upon disconnection, stored tokens are removed.
